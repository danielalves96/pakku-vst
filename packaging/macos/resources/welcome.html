Pakku — multiband transient shaper

This installer places Pakku in the system plug-in folders. Pick the formats you want on the next screen.

  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

Universal binary, Apple Silicon and Intel. Requires macOS 11 or later. Restart your host afterwards — most only scan for plug-ins on startup.

Free and open source, made in Brazil by Kyantech Labs.
